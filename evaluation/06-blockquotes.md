# Blockquotes

## Simple quote

> The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
> Second line of the same quote.

## Lazy continuation

> This quote
continues without a marker on the second line (lazy continuation).

## Nested quotes

> Outer quote.
>
> > Inner quote.
> >
> > > Third level deep.

## Quotes containing other blocks

> ## A heading inside a quote
>
> - A list item inside a quote
> - Another item
>
> ```js
> // code inside a quote
> const quoted = true;
> ```
>
> | Even | A  |
> | ---- | -- |
> | tiny | ✓  |
>
> Final paragraph with **bold** and *italic*.

## Separated quotes

> First quote block.

> Second quote block — separated by a blank line; some renderers merge these, CommonMark keeps them apart.

## Quote with hard break

> Line one  
> Line two after a hard break (two trailing spaces above).
